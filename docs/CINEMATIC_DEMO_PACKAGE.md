# VYTA — Cinematic Product Demonstration Film Package

**Product**: VYTA — Your Body. Your Rhythm.  
**Project**: Human-Sensing Wellness Experience built for the Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge  
**Target Duration**: 2 minutes 30 seconds (150 seconds)  
**Format**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9), 30 FPS, MP4 (H.264 / AAC)  
**Tone & Aesthetic**: Obsidian forest, luminous emerald & sage, calm, human-centered, scientifically disciplined, high-end technology launch.

---

## 1. Creative Concept

The film opens in quiet, contemplative darkness — a deliberate departure from aggressive, noisy tech product videos. It anchors on a relatable tension: our devices record countless metrics, yet our lived bodily state remains opaque. 

VYTA is introduced not as another dashboard of overwhelming numbers, but as an intentional human-sensing space. The visual narrative transitions from the chaos of modern digital noise into the calm, organic breathing canvas of VYTA, moving through informed consent, deterministic breathing cycles (4-2-6s), and the rigorous engineering of real-time camera sensing via the Presage SmartSpectra SDK.

The video balances emotional resonance with technical integrity: real UI recordings, genuine camera pipelines, honest disclosure of optical sensing parameters, and clean typography set to ambient cinematic electronics.

1. Set project to `1920x1080`, `30.000 fps`, `Rec.709-A`.
2. Place screen recordings on `V2` (apply subtle 1.05x -> 1.08x scale drift).
3. Place title overlays on `V3` (SemiBold, +50 tracking).
4. Audio: Voiceover on `A1` with Fairlight Dialogue Leveller; Music on `A2` with -3.5 dB auto-ducking on speech.

1. Canvas: `16:9` (`1920x1080`), `30 FPS`.
2. Add screen recordings as Overlay with 12px rounded corner mask.
3. Apply "Slow Zoom" (3% over 10s) to keep visual engagement high.
