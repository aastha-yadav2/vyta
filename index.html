Understanding the Technology

How Contactless Sensing Works

VYTA uses a compatible optical camera and subtle light variance algorithms to attempt to detect physiological signals without any wearables.

1

Position in Frame

Position yourself comfortably in front of your camera, centered within the frame at a natural sitting distance.

2

Balanced Ambient Light

Sit comfortably with your face evenly illuminated by ambient room or screen light. Avoid direct harsh backlighting.

3

Signal Evaluation

Begin a session and allow the sensor to evaluate signal clarity. The sensor requires stability to measure accurately.

ℹ
No Guaranteed Measurements: Optical signal quality depends on camera frame rates, ambient room lighting, facial stillness, and hardware compatibility. VYTA is a wellness awareness tool, not a medical device.
Transparent & On-Device

Privacy & Informed Consent

We believe physiological wellness technology must be built on verifiable trust and total user control.

🔒
100% On-Device Processing

All camera frames are processed strictly in volatile local memory by the native C++ SmartSpectra engine. Video is never uploaded, streamed, or stored remotely.

📹
Session-Based Camera Power

The camera is active only when you explicitly start a session. Tracks are completely powered down and stopped immediately when you stop or reset.

💾
Minimal Local-Only History

Only session duration, timestamps, and computed numerical averages are saved in your local computer storage. No facial images or biometric vectors are ever created or saved.

⏹
Complete User Control

You can pause or end any session at any moment. All recorded history can be cleared or exported with a single click in Settings.

Optical Ergonomics

Camera Positioning & Environment

Proper positioning ensures the optical sensor can evaluate micro-color changes clearly.

💡
Stylized placement guide. Not an active facial scan or biometric recognition.
✓
Distance (~18–36 inches)

Sit comfortably at arm's length so your head and upper chest fill the frame naturally.

✓
Even Frontal Light

Face an ambient lamp or soft screen light. Avoid bright windows directly behind you (backlighting).

✓
Clear Visibility

Keep your forehead and cheeks unobstructed by hats or hands during sensing.

✓
Relaxed Stillness

Breathe normally. Minimizing excessive head movement allows the sensor to detect subtle rhythm.
